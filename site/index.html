How it works

Straight answers.

Why is there no Connect Wallet button?

Quantus wallets live on your phone and use post-quantum ML-DSA keys that browser wallets like MetaMask cannot sign. You mint by sending QTC from the Quantus Wallet app. No signature on this site is ever needed.

How is ownership recorded?

Each finalized payment to the treasury is an on-chain fact. The indexer replays those transfers in order and assigns punks from the shuffled list, so anyone running the open-source indexer gets the same owners.

How do I send a punk to someone?

Write a remark on chain from the wallet that holds it. With the Quantus CLI:

quantus call --pallet System --call remark_with_event --args '["qpunks:transfer:1234:qzRECIPIENT"]' --from my_wallet

Several punks at once: qpunks:transfer:12,34,56:qz…. The transfer applies only if your wallet owns every listed punk.

What about the 10% royalty?

Quantus has no contracts, so nothing can force a royalty on private trades. The 10% applies to every sale on the QuantusPunks marketplace, which opens after sell-out.

What if I send the wrong amount?

You get as many whole punks as you paid for, up to 20 per transfer. Anything left over, or anything sent after sell-out, goes on the refund list and is sent back to the same address.

Can I check the shuffle was fair?

Yes. The hash of the trait file and the hash of the shuffle seed are published before the mint.
